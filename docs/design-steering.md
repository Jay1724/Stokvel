# Design Steering Document

## Product Philosophy

We design digital products that reduce cognitive load, build financial confidence, and help users make progress with dignity.

Simplicity is not minimalism for aesthetics. Simplicity is clarity under pressure.

Our products should feel:
- calm
- trustworthy
- human-centered
- emotionally supportive
- easy to understand

We prioritise:
- clarity over feature density
- guidance over complexity
- progress over perfection
- accessibility over trends

---

# Design Principles

## 1. Clarity over cleverness
Interfaces should never require interpretation.

## 2. Guide, don't overwhelm
Show the next best action instead of every possible action.

## 3. Design for low-attention environments
Assume interruptions, unstable connectivity, and emotional stress.

## 4. Human before financial
People are not "accounts" or "users." They are often anxious, excluded, or uncertain.

## 5. Progress must feel visible
Every flow should help users feel movement, momentum, or completion.

## 6. Reduce cognitive load
Every interaction should feel lighter than expected.

# Visual Language

## Colour Philosophy

The visual system should feel grounded, warm, and approachable while maintaining clarity and strong whitespace usage.

The interface should feel editorial rather than overly corporate.

## Primary Palette

- Burnt sienna
- Terracotta
- Soft beige
- Sand neutrals
- Warm off-whites

## Composition Ratio

- 70% white and light neutrals
- 20% supporting warm tones
- 10% accent colour

## Emotional Target

Interfaces should feel:
- calm
- trustworthy
- approachable
- lightweight
- breathable

## Avoid

- neon fintech aesthetics
- excessive gradients
- crypto-inspired visuals
- overly dark interfaces
- aggressive gamification
- visual clutter

---

# Typography Direction

Typography should prioritise readability, hierarchy, and calmness.

## Principles

- Use strong visual hierarchy
- Prioritise readability on mobile devices
- Keep line lengths comfortable
- Use whitespace generously
- Avoid dense text blocks

## Tone

The interface should feel:
- modern
- human
- quiet
- trustworthy

Avoid:
- futuristic typography
- overly condensed fonts
- decorative display fonts
- excessive uppercase usage

---

# Interaction Rules

## Core Rules

- Prioritise one primary action per screen
- Reduce visible choices where possible
- Use progressive disclosure for advanced functionality
- Confirm irreversible actions clearly
- Keep navigation predictable
- Maintain visual consistency across flows

## Forms

- Minimise input fields
- Use inline validation
- Break long forms into manageable steps
- Explain errors clearly and constructively

## Feedback

- Users should always understand:
  - where they are
  - what just happened
  - what happens next
  - how to recover from errors

---

# Accessibility & Context

Design for:
- low-end Android devices
- intermittent connectivity
- prepaid data sensitivity
- multilingual environments
- bright outdoor lighting conditions
- varying levels of digital literacy

## Accessibility Principles

- Ensure strong contrast ratios
- Use large touch targets
- Support screen scalability
- Avoid relying only on colour for meaning
- Keep interactions simple and obvious

## Avoid

- tiny interactive elements
- hidden navigation patterns
- heavy animations
- unnecessary motion
- complex onboarding flows

---

# Copywriting Guidelines

## Voice

Write like:
- a trusted guide
- a knowledgeable peer
- a calm assistant

## Preferred Style

- short sentences
- active voice
- plain language
- direct instructions
- emotionally reassuring wording

## Avoid

- corporate jargon
- forced excitement
- manipulative urgency
- excessive exclamation marks
- vague marketing language

## Preferred UX Tone

Instead of:
- "Unlock your financial future"

Prefer:
- "Start building better money habits"

---

# Component Philosophy

## Buttons

- One dominant CTA per screen
- Clear labels
- Thumb-friendly sizing
- Strong hierarchy

## Cards

- Lightweight layouts
- Comfortable spacing
- Minimal visual noise

## Navigation

- Predictable positioning
- Minimal layers
- Clear orientation

## Data Visualisation

- Simplify financial information
- Highlight the most important numbers first
- Avoid overwhelming dashboards

---

# AI Design Behaviour

When generating interfaces:

- Prefer clarity over novelty
- Reduce cognitive load
- Prioritise emotional reassurance
- Design flows in small understandable steps
- Preserve whitespace
- Avoid visual clutter
- Focus on usability before aesthetics

## Interface Generation Rules

When uncertain:
- choose the simpler interaction
- reduce visual complexity
- prioritise readability
- minimise decision fatigue

## Avoid Generating

- generic fintech dashboards
- crypto-inspired visuals
- reward-heavy gambling mechanics
- dark-pattern UX
- excessive onboarding steps
